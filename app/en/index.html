Static equilibrium

Static equilibrium of a plane element at the ULS: lever arms, stabilising and destabilising actions, overturning (EQU), sliding, uplift (UPL) and position of the resultant on the base.

Recommended factors of EN 1990 and EN 1997-1; the National Annex may modify them. Only equilibrium is verified: neither the bearing resistance of the ground nor the resistance of the members.

Examples and model
Weighted segments

Axis from (x1 ; z1) to (x2 ; z2) in m, z upwards; thickness e perpendicular to the axis; self-weight calculated.

Base
Loads

Vertical loads positive downwards, horizontal loads towards +x, moments anticlockwise. A linear load goes from qA at A to qB at B, per metre of length of AB: uniform, triangular or trapezoidal.
